# Brand Overview

Tahche is a recruitment-outsourcing BPO from the Philippines, for talent acquisition and business
expansion. Three keywords drove the brand's design, straight from the Brand Book:

<div class="ds-demo flex flex-wrap gap-3">
  <span class="rounded-md bg-secondary-500 px-6 py-4 text-paragraph-medium-500 text-neutral-900">Friendly</span>
  <span class="rounded-md bg-primary-500 px-6 py-4 text-paragraph-medium-500 text-white">Reliable</span>
  <span class="rounded-md bg-brand-red px-6 py-4 text-paragraph-medium-500 text-white">Competitive</span>
</div>

These design characters aim for a more friendly approach while staying competitive and
professional — the same balance [Design Principles](/philosophies/principles) asks of the
product UI, carried into brand terms: warm but professional, confident, action-oriented.

## The logo

<img src="/brand/cover-01.png" alt="Tahche logo" class="mx-auto my-8 max-w-md rounded-md border border-neutral-200" />

The mark combines three elements, each with a specific meaning:

- **The globe** (yellow, crossed lines) — Tahche's services rendered across the globe.
- **The cheetah** (blue, head and body) — Tahche's main character, standing for speed and
  reliability.
- **10 small triangular patterns** on the cheetah's body — Tahche's 10-year anniversary.

The logo was redesigned to be more straightforward, modern, and professional than its
predecessor.
