# Brand Overview

Tahche is a recruitment-outsourcing BPO from the Philippines, for talent acquisition and business
expansion.

## The mark

<img src="/brand/logo-lockup-horizontal.png" alt="Tahche logo — cheetah cub mark and TAHCHE wordmark" class="mx-auto my-8 max-w-xl rounded-md border border-neutral-200" />

The mark is a reclining cheetah cub — rendered in the brand's Blue spots over Yellow fur by
default, or in one of five other approved color treatments (see [Logo](/brand/logo)) — paired
with the bold **TAHCHE** wordmark. There's no globe or anniversary iconography in this version:
the mark was simplified down to the cheetah itself, standing for speed and reliability.
